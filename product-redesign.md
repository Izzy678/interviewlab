Re-think the product experience, landing page, dashboard, interview setup, and navigation based on the following product vision.

--------------------------------------------------------
PRODUCT VISION
--------------------------------------------------------

This product is NOT an AI chatbot.

This product is NOT a generic interview question generator.

This product is NOT another SaaS dashboard.

The product is an AI-native Interview Workspace.

The goal is to make candidates feel like they have stepped into a real interview studio where they can prepare for and experience realistic voice interviews.

The conversation itself is the product.

The interface should disappear into the background and let the interview experience become the focus.

The AI should feel like a professional interviewer, not an assistant.

The entire experience should communicate:

calm
premium
professional
focused
confident
high trust
modern
voice-first

Avoid making the experience playful, gimmicky or overly futuristic.

--------------------------------------------------------
DESIGN INSPIRATION
--------------------------------------------------------

Take inspiration from the product philosophy behind:

• Notion
• Superhuman
• Attio

Not because of their exact layouts, but because of their design principles.

The application should feel like a modern workspace instead of a traditional SaaS dashboard.

Large spacing.

Minimal UI.

Excellent typography.

Purposeful animations.

Everything should feel intentional.

Avoid:

- generic AI illustrations
- robot mascots
- stock AI graphics
- polygon robot faces
- glowing cyberpunk visuals
- cluttered dashboards
- excessive gradients
- too many cards
- information overload

--------------------------------------------------------

Analyzing resume...

--------------------------------------------------------
LIVE INTERVIEW EXPERIENCE
--------------------------------------------------------

The interview should become the hero of the product.

The user should never see the generated interview plan or question list.

That information should remain internal.

Instead the interface should focus entirely on the live conversation.

The interview page should feel like sitting across from a real interviewer.

The AI interviewer should:

• greet the candidate
• introduce the interview
• create a small icebreaker
• naturally move into the interview
• ask follow-up questions dynamically
• remember previous answers
• react conversationally
• request clarification when needed

The UI should remain minimal.

Show only information that supports the conversation.

Possible UI elements:

• AI interviewer state
• Candidate speaking state
• Live transcript
• Voice waveform
• Current interview stage
• Time remaining
• End interview button

Avoid excessive buttons.

Avoid wizard-like interfaces.

Avoid making the interview feel like filling out a form.

--------------------------------------------------------
POST INTERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------

After the interview finishes, the AI should conclude naturally before showing the report.

Do not abruptly switch screens.

Fade into the interview analysis.

The report should feel like feedback from a real interviewer rather than AI-generated analytics.

--------------------------------------------------------
MOTION DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------

Use subtle animations throughout the experience.

Examples:

• breathing cards
• soft transitions
• live transcript typing
• waveform animations
• pulsing speaking indicators
• smooth page transitions
• elegant loading states

Animations should reinforce the feeling that the application is alive.

Never use animations simply because they look cool.

--------------------------------------------------------
OVERALL GOAL
--------------------------------------------------------

The application should feel less like an AI tool and more like entering a premium interview studio.

Every design decision should support immersion, trust, professionalism and focus.

The user should leave thinking:
